# The Tips Ledger

Two tips, one proof each.

| Tip | Proof |
|---|---|
| 1 Steer it, queue it | A /steer changed a running turn without stopping it, and three /queue prompts ran in order after it, one of them edited and one removed with /queue list, edit and rm |
| 2 Work the board | A goal became cards with real assignees; parallel cards finished on inexpensive profiles; a child card started with its parents' results; a reviewer you named checked the draft before you did |

> ✅ **What you have now**
>
> Two habits that change how Hermes feels. You no longer stop a run to fix it or wait for it to finish before asking for the next thing: you steer it and queue behind it. And you no longer hand Hermes a project as one long chat: you hand it a goal, read the cards it writes, say go, and come back to finished work and the few questions that were yours to answer.
